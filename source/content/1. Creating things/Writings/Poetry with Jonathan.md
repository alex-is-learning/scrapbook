- Me and Jono are experimenting with writing poetry weekly, where each week has a theme/format
# Week 1
- Quatrains with iambic tetrameter
- E.g., [Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/42891/stopping-by-woods-on-a-snowy-evening) by Robert Frost

## 1
We toss and turn each night for weeks
The pain of sleeping on the sheets
Of beds we share and do not own 
It's better than to be alone
## 2
Each cigarette is bad for me 
A pounding head and shaking knees
There must be ways to ease the pain 
That don't involve a poisoned brain
## 3 
- Friday 2025-10-17 

There was a cutie at the till
A flirty smile and looks that kill 
It has been nice to see the change 
Since I bought my thrifted chain
And stepped into a brighter frame 
The light shines forth with want and will
