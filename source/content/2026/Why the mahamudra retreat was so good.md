## Mahamudra retreat
- Recently I did a Mahamudra retreat and it was ridiculously good
- Fuck it I started writing about what was so good about it but I'll make a separate page
- Tibetan Buddhism is essentially Buddhism 3.0
- Up until this point the main Buddhism I'd been aware of and vaguely oriented towards (even if just by intention) was Buddhism 1.0 (Theravada)
- Turns out a proper Mahamudra retreat is orders of magnitude better than e.g.
	1. Piecing things together via twitter (tpot[^1])
	2. Paying twitter meditation coaches (will be far less pedagologically rigorous than a whole-ass team and a whole-ass lineage, of course)
	3. Trying to learn via books
# Numbered brain-dump
1. Wildly efficient re: concentration ("whole of the elephant path in 2 days but also a pointing out that you don't need anything like perfect concentration. Love the "candle in the wind looking at cave paintings, just need to reduce the wind enough to be able to see the paintings more clearly" thing)
2. Amazing pedagogy, e.g. the elephant path, many memetically fit metaphors/analogies, the 4 yogas (concentration → emptiness → non-duality → non-meditation)
3. How they essentially hypnotise you and use good suggestive language to really encourage you to just do the stuff and believe that you can
4. How they have a big focus on how this isn't complicated and doesn't need to take a long time
5. The 7 points of posture helping a lot with comfort and concentration
6. The long Q&A portions making it so that people can ask dumb questions and reduce doubt
7. Their ambition ("this is about enlightenment")
