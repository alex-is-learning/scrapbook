So then -- why no triumphant horns? Why no celebrant blaring?

Should not the freedom taste divine? 

My life is a smouldering ruin. I destroyed myself for nothing.

But not just myself; I've burned the ones who loved me. 

I marched forth with a burning torch, to burn away all cruft. When a loved one put a hand on my shoulder, or try to soothe my broken heart, I'd burn them too. To one who believes that the now is inadequate, the soothings of others ring of empty promise, of deception, an entreaty to leave the quest behind and be merely ordinary, merely broken, like the rest.

Your love cannot touch me, I do not need it, do not desire it. I must slay the dragon. I do not need you, you are not destined for greatness as I am. Do not distract me, do not waste my time. Can't you see I'm on the march? 

And sometimes, confusion, as the love of another leads to deep refuge. I can't make sense of this - why does this feel so good? What is this feeling of presence and wholeness? This has nothing to do with the quest. I cannot make sense of this.

And then, I burn them, the spell is broken, I rediscover my brokenness, resume the march. 

I see now, that they were right, and I was wrong. Snap out of it, be with us. Be with love, give and receive. My language is clumsy, to describe such uncharted territory, I am near illiterate in this land, but I will learn. My heart is open now. 

I weep to behold the ashen wasteland of my own creation. What is done is done, and I see flowers before me.
