# Prompting an LLM - "what does it mean"

In astrology, **Mercury is the "ruler" of Virgo**, meaning it’s exceptionally happy and powerful in this sign. If Mercury represents _how_ we ==think and communicate==, Virgo provides the ==precision== instruments to do it perfectly.

Think of Mercury in Virgo as the ultimate "quality control" officer of the zodiac.
### Key Characteristics

When Mercury is in Virgo, communication moves away from big, abstract ideas and focuses on the **practical, the tangible, and the detailed.**

- **Precision & Accuracy:** People with this placement (or during this transit) hate vagueness. They don't just want the answer; they want the data, the footnotes, and the correct spelling.
    
- **Analytical Prowess:** This is the placement of the "editor." It excels at breaking down complex systems into small, manageable parts to see how they work.
    
- **Service-Oriented:** Communication is often used as a tool to be helpful. "I'm not criticizing you; I'm helping you optimize," is a very Mercury in Virgo sentiment.
    
- **Pragmatism:** They aren't interested in "what if" unless it leads to "how to."
