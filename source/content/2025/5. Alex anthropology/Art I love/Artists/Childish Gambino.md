# "Awaken, My Love!" - 2016
- ![[1762897393143.webp]]
- The first time I heard the lead single, I was in the library with two friends, I put my headphones on and was fucking BLOWN AWAY
- (Me and Your Mama). Insanely different sound from Because the Internet, came out of absolutely nowhere, absolutely incredible. Goosebumps 
- And then the whole album came out and omg, what a fucking mixed bag, holy shit. Have Some Love, Boogieman, Zombies, California, (*especially* California) are *so fucking corny*
- But some incredible highlights too, ok mainly Redbone and Me and Your Mama, but still, what fucking highlights
- Overall I was kinda disappointed but impressed by the mega-highlights and the major change of pace. I also tried listening to Maggot Brain to understand the references
# This is America - 2018
- At this point I definitely felt distanced from Gambino, it felt clear that he's like... not a guy, he's a character, or *something*, idk.
- Really cool to see this get so huge though. Music video is insane, this really was such a cultural moment
# 3.15.20 - 2020
- Similar feeling - it came out, it seemed like he didn't really care about it that much, it had some good songs (the 21 Savage one and the final song, which is INSANE), but I struggled to care that much
	- The final song, which, since he released the album and actually named the songs is now called Final Church, is just *obscenely* good, it's actually fucking insane. I love his crazy preacher character, so so so charismatic and powerful
	- I literally can't listen to this song without dancing, and the urge to dance rarely comes to me lol. The build up at the end is just absolutely insane
