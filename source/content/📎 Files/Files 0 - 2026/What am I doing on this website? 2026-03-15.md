## Reply
This is a great question, and I think you're right to sense that "writing on my scrapbook" undersells what's actually happening. A few terms come to mind that might fit better than "doing philosophy," depending on which angle resonates most.

**Sensemaking** is probably the closest single word. It captures the active, ongoing nature of what you're doing — you're not just passively consuming ideas (like Sasha's post), you're metabolizing them, annotating them, testing them against your own experience, and trying to arrive at something personally actionable. It's a term used in knowledge management and complexity theory, but it also just describes what you're literally doing: making sense of things.

**Personal epistemics** or **applied philosophy** might work if you want to emphasize the rigor side — you're not doing armchair philosophy in the abstract, you're trying to figure out what's actually true about how to live well, and then live accordingly. The document you uploaded is a perfect example: you're taking Sasha's tanha/chanda framework, cross-referencing it with Heidegger's ready-at-hand/present-at-hand, checking it against your own guitar-learning experience, noticing where Claude's generated "first principles" fell flat — that's genuinely philosophical work, just applied to your own life rather than written for a journal.

**Reflective practice** is another option, borrowed from education and professional development. It foregrounds the iterative loop: experience → reflection → updated model → different action → new experience.

A few more that might land depending on your mood: **life engineering** (which Sasha himself uses), **self-authorship** (you're literally writing yourself into a more coherent worldview), or even just **inquiry** — simple, unpretentious, and accurate.

The 80 minutes is the tell. That's chanda, not tanha. You're not grinding through this because you think you should have a website. You're pulled into the activity of clarifying your own thinking. The writing is the vehicle, but the thing you're actually doing is something like _figuring out how to live_ — and that's worth naming accurately.
