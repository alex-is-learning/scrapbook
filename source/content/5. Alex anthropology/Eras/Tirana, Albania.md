- [[Era 16 - Back to Europe, Christmas at home]]

So, I landed my role at Refract, and then flew from Taipei to Albania because [River Kenna](https://x.com/the_wilderless?ref_src=twsrc%5Egoogle%7Ctwcamp%5Eserp%7Ctwgr%5Eauthor) recommended visiting him in Tirana. 

I stayed there a month and only met up with him once because I was socially anxious as hell (you know me), but I also got to meet [Michael Ashcroft](https://x.com/m_ashcroft) and [Alexandria Unknown Surname](https://x.com/innerpathing), and I also just really enjoyed my solitary time for a month, HUGELY enjoying the temperate Mediterranean autumn, eating at cheap restaurants, working at a coworking space, going to the gym... it was honestly really awesome. 
I mostly:
1. Ate food (really nice to have Italian/Greek-type food after being in Asia for ~6 months)
2. Worked on [[Job - Refract]] stuff from my coworking space
3. Listened to comedy podcats (Review Revue, to this day I am obsessed with Geoffrey James from Headgum)
4. Walked around

It was awesome.
I made 0 friends and basically didn't talk to any IRL, but it was a very nice chill month
