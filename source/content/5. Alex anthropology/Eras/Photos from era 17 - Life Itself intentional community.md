Asking ChatGPT: `I had a cake in france which I think was about it being new years, maybe - Galette ?`:

Yes! You’re thinking of **Galette des Rois**, the French king cake traditionally eaten around **Epiphany (January 6th)**, though it often extends into New Year’s celebrations. It’s a flaky, buttery puff pastry filled with **frangipane** (a rich almond cream) and sometimes other variations like chocolate or apple.

**Key Features of a Galette des Rois:**

• **Puff pastry**: Golden, flaky, and buttery.
• **Frangipane filling**: Made from ground almonds, butter, sugar, and eggs.
• **Hidden fève**: A small figurine (often porcelain) is baked inside.
• **Paper crown**: The person who finds the fève in their slice is crowned **king or queen** for the day.
