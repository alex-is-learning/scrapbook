- [[08. Enneagram 3 thinking sins]]

- 3 core = build/achieve; 
- 4 wing = depth, artistry, originality; 
- result = “the producer/creator who wants the work to be both excellent and true.”

# **Core dynamic**

- **Core drive (3):** “Be valuable by achieving.” Orient to goals, image, effectiveness, results.
- **Wing color (4):** “But it must be _authentically me_.” Craves depth, originality, aesthetic integrity.
- **Tension:** Image-management vs. authenticity. You want to win **and** be real.
  
# **Typical traits**
- More **introspective, refined, and private** than 3w2; less chatty, more curated.
- Competitive in **creative/intellectual** lanes (art, design, writing, branding, research).
- Strong sense of **taste**; cares about the _coherence_ of a body of work.
- Can oscillate between **high output** and **moody withdrawal** (4-ish dips).
# **What it feels like from the inside**
- “If I’m not producing, who am I?” (3)
- “If what I’m producing isn’t true to me, what’s the point?” (4)
- Prone to **impostor syndrome** and a hyper-attuned “is this fake?” radar—especially for yourself.
# **Strengths (when healthy)**
- **Excellence with soul:** you ship work that’s both high-quality and personally meaningful.
- **Brand sense:** you can frame/position ideas and make them land.
- **Adaptive grit:** you iterate, learn fast, and keep standards high.
# **Pitfalls (when stuck)**
- **Vanity/deceit (3):** smoothing or spinning the story—first to yourself—so it “looks good.”
- **Envy/comparison (4):** the “someone else has the _real_ voice” ache.
- **Over-identifying with output:** self-worth rises/falls with metrics, praise, momentum.
# **3w4 vs close neighbors**
- **3w4 vs 4w3:**
    - 3w4 leads with _outcomes_ → then asks, “is it me?”
    - 4w3 leads with _==authentic expression==_ → then asks, “can I get it seen?”
    - Litmus: What stings more—**not being successful** (3) or **being seen but misunderstood** (4)?
- **3w4 vs 3w2:**
    - 3w4 = polished, selective, artistic/professional.
    - 3w2 = outgoing, network-savvy, people-forward.
# **Subtype flavor (if you track them)**
- **sx-dom 3w4:** intense, magnetic, “make _the_ definitive statement,” prone to image swings.
- **so-dom 3w4:** prestige-aware, platform-builder, careful curation of reputation.
- **sp-dom 3w4:** quality/workmanship focus, understated, “let the craft speak.”
# **Growth moves (practical)**
- **Truth reps:** Daily “What’s actually true vs. what looks good?” (3’s virtue = **truthfulness/authenticity**.)
- **Process goals:** Track inputs (hours, drafts) alongside outcomes (views, wins) to uncouple worth from metrics.
- **Repair > polish:** After a stumble, narrate the _uncut_ version to one trusted person—build tolerance for being seen mid-process.
- **Envy alchemy:** When envy spikes, name the _quality you admire_ (e.g., bravery, rigor). Set one concrete practice to build it.
- **Scheduled emptiness:** Time blocks with no performing/optimizing; let identity breathe off-stage.
# **In relationships**
- Super supportive when aligned; can slide into **coach/PR manager** mode.
- Growth = letting yourself be loved **when not impressive**, and letting others be messy **without optimizing them**.
# **Work lanes that suit the pattern**

- Any role mixing **creation + presentation**: design, writing, product, research synthesis, branding, content, entrepreneurship.
    
- You’ll thrive where **standards + originality** are prized, not just raw speed.
