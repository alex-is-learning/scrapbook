- I started out by writing my default stories, like you know, "me and my dad didn't connect and this made me feel like I was boring". It's been really nice as I've been working on this project to actually fully remove the old negative formulations, so the below list is just the new positive formulations. So I don't have to wade through loads of "ok this is the truth, this is why my situation is bad, in a huge amount of detail, and now here's an attempt at a positive reframe". It's like, nope, here are the truths of my situation, boom, they're all positive. I'm very lucky. 
# I have been profoundly lucky
- 👆 This is the new meta-story. After working on this for days, this is what I started to converge on, and it feels really good and correct!
