- [[Mythopoesis 1 - same life story, different conclusions]]

- E.g., explaining my relationship history to Jono and his two friends, "oh it's lame because x", "huh, it's surprising to me that you think it's lame, I'd say that actually it's good/nice/etc"
## Having a girlfriend at 15 was a profound oasis and deeply meaningful
- Don't feel like I need to write about this, feels very obvious. My home life was tough, and then omg, amazing girlfriend in my bedroom, suddenly life is amazing. And we'd drive to school in her car, share music, go on holidays etc. It was so so good
## My girlfriend was the absolute best thing in my life so of course I wanted to go to university with her
- God damn it, this made me cry just to write, damn
- Again, no need to write more. To not go to university with her would have been absolutely mad, seriously shooting myself in the foot. It's not like she was a random meh girlfriend and I went to uni with her despite not really wanting to. I really really wanted to be with her
## In a lot of ways, getting back together after our year apart felt very right
- It didn't feel as easy and straightforward → we'd been apart from a year, and it'd been a *hard year*, my master's year was painful in various ways. There were more adult fears of like, "getting back together feels very *serious*, can I guarantee it'll work out", etc
- But also, it felt really good in a bunch of ways, and I loved e.g. renting our place together, being together during COVID, getting our dog
## I endorse not being involved in our house-buying process
- At the time it was like, yeah, buying a house is what you do, it's an investment, it's better than renting, etc
- And not being opinionated on what the place looked like, trusting in my girlfriend's taste (which was very good)
- It was a no brainer to buy a house, at the time it felt like we were gonna be together forever. It's a starter home, it gets you on the ladder, etc. What are we gonna do, keep renting?
## We had to break up!
- This feels like another just total no-brainer that doesn't need to be written about. It worked out very well for both of us in the long run, look at us now! But also, look at us then, it's been good this whole time innit
