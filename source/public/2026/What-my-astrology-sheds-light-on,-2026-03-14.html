  • “Wahhh astrology isn’t real!” - shut up nerd, it sheds light on genuine patterns of mine ♌️♉️♏️

Sun in Leo

  • I love attention
  • I love to lead (?)
  • I love to be creative!
  • This feels very true to me. And it has been hidden for a long time, since I was 18 really, behind the Scorpio rising. I went to uni and boom, socially anxious as hell. But before that, very Leo (with my group of friends at school). And post-Kensho, I’ve been reconnecting with my Leo-ness, especially since living at the EA hotel from October 2025 and having a safe and supportive community with a core team of people who really love me

Moon in Taurus

  • I am grounded, emotionally
  • And very stable, day-to-day. I wake up in the exact same cheerful mood every day, lol. I don’t have big mood swings, and I can let things go very quickly (a lot of this really boosted by Kensho and my post-rationalist healing arc))
  • I actually don’t want to do long talks about feelings (vs say a water sign moon). People who want to do that seem pretty melodramatic to me
  • I love stability and building from a grounded place (I’m actually triple fixed, sun/moon/rising, which feels true to my experience - I don’t think I’m an ideas guy, I’m much more of a COO than a CEO, I think)

Rising = Scorpio

  • Scorpio rising → I’ve had a tumultuous life arc and this feels very important to me. A lot of profundity in this path! A connection to the suffering of the working class, the unfairness of how birth family affects you so much, how much effort it takes to overcome trauma (and how there’s a point at which you are very unlikely to overcome your trauma, if you have n amount, like my adopted sister)
  • This feels like a unique gift, in a way. Not sure what to do with it, how to channel it. But I’ve been approaching it, I think

Mercury in Virgo

  • Mercury in Virgo → I am very into thinking, meta-cognition, visual notes, clarity of thinking, planning things well, improving my ability to think, etc
  • Are visual notes underrated and poorly understood?
  • I’m very good at this, in a self-trained way. Back in ~2022, at the biotech job

Ugly art

Made by Nano Banana 2.0

Here’s the breakdown of what each element in the artwork symbolises:

The Solar Lion (centre) — The blazing Leo Sun sits at the heart of everything: a crowned lion’s face within a radiant golden sun, surrounded by eight corona rays. Gold and deep amber dominate. Leo rules the self, the stage, the sovereign heart — nothing hides here.

The Taurus Moon (left crescent) — A warm, earthy crescent in deep gold with bull’s horns subtly etched into its surface. Roses bloom from the dark soil below it. Taurus Moon anchors all that Leo fire in the body: sensory pleasure, loyalty, a fierce need for peace and beauty.

The Scorpio Rising mask (right) — A dark violet ceremonial mask with glowing crimson eyes, a coiling scorpion tail above it, and phoenix feathers below — because Scorpio Rising is the face others see first: intense, magnetic, slightly unknowable. The rebirth feathers speak to Scorpio’s transformative core.

Venus in Gemini — twin butterflies — Two butterflies in contrasting palettes (teal-violet and rose-pink) mirror each other across the canvas, light and mercurial. Venus in Gemini loves through words, wit, and duality — always two stories running at once.

Pisces Mars — deep-swimming fish — In the misty water below, two fish swim in opposite directions, bound by a dotted thread. Pisces Mars acts from intuition rather than aggression — its drive dissolves into currents, dreams, and compassion.

The faint zodiac wheel and constellation lines tie it all together, while the dark earth base and starry sky hold the whole cosmic scene.
