2. Thinking

Role model of “Metacognition” (“thinking about your thinking, thinking about your process”)

  • The deliberateness with which you approach life, thinking, learning, work etc is special
  • At least in part one of your superpowers: deliberateness in thinking, thinking about thinking up the stack
  • Claim: this is a distinct superpower from rapid learning abilities
    • You can learn a bunch of object-level stuff but not be trying to encorporate into all levels of life and thinking
  • I think its partly whats responsible for the number of times you’ve been able to redefine your responsibilities & ownership successfully
  • What I notice which is unique to you is how far “up the stack” your self-improvement goes.
    • Meaning not just trying to get better at some particular subject matter or skill, but being deliberate about improving your approach and way of thinking.
    • Creative / optimising but at really high level things

Lucidity (mental clarity, ability to make things clear and structured)

  • Impressive ability to crystallise, structure and organise information
  • Historically you haven’t celebrated this enough
  • sounds like you think it’s easy to do this and therefore replace-able, not valuable
  • Counter-example: the surprise [manager] felt after having a few conversations re: [project] into something very legible, crystallised it
  • Even has a guy currently working on this with 25 years experience and doesn’t seem to be able to do the job like I could, I would have grok-d more and put it in a more legible format
    • 🚨 🚨 !!!
  • Therefore this isn't trivial and should be done more!
  • “You blew my mind a few times with our early collaborations, this was really valuable.”

Learning like a champ

  • You’re a monster at devouring information lol
  • Seen this across lots of domains
  • Talking to you week to week can be disorienting because of how much you’ve been able to learn and adapt your mental models since we spoke the last time. So crazy how fast you get up to speed
  • “what Alex am I talking to this week”
  • May have read a big book, devoured some info
