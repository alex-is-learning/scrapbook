Era 15 - Asia

Me and Simmo worked on our dumb youtube channel and I lived in various Airbnbs and explored

I much preferred Taipei to Manilla, the greenery was incredibly welcome after the harsh concrete & sun of Manilla, gosh. I made a really good friend (Tilly!!), dated a bit, did some extra-curriculars (group workouts in the park, open mic nights and shit), explored the city, etc. It’s a very very vibey place.


I was hella smitten with Taipei immediately - soooo much greener than Manila, and so dense and interesting and just fucking vibe-y, cool neon signs everywhere and shit
